RIFTLINE

TACTICAL SHOOTER · PROTOTYPE

Built-in map. Or load your own .glb map (it stays on your computer).

This game needs a keyboard and mouse.

Attackers plant the Charge on site A or B. Defenders stop them or defuse it. First to 7 rounds wins. Sides swap after round 6.

  • W A S D move
  • Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB scope
  • Shift walk (accurate) · Ctrl/C crouch
  • Space jump · R reload
  • 1 primary · 2 sidearm · Wheel swap
  • B buy menu (buy phase) · Tab scoreboard
  • F hold to plant / defuse · Esc pause

Tip: stop moving before you shoot. Rifles are accurate on the first bullet, and pull down to control the spray.
